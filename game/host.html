The rules

Build. Every question has a timer (60 seconds unless you change it). Answer right and every second left on the clock is one base brick. Base bricks times your multiplier go on your castle.

Multiplier. Everyone starts at ×1. Right answer: it goes up by 1. Wrong or no answer: it goes down by 1 (never below ×1). No cap.

Attack. After each question, pick a target on the leaderboard. If you answer the next question right, your target loses seconds left × 10 bricks. Your streak multiplier does not count on attacks. Gang up on whoever is close to winning! Castle walls stop most of it: a castle loses at most 4% of its bricks per question, no matter how many players attack it.

Final Round. Word problems: base bricks are doubled.

Win. The first castle to reach 15,000 bricks wins. If nobody gets there, the biggest castle wins.

Tip: test with one Chromebook before class. Open the student link, join, and make sure the name appears here.
