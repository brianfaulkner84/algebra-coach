The rules

Build. Every question has a timer (60 seconds unless you change it). Answer right and every second left on the clock is one base brick. Base bricks times your multiplier go on your castle.

Multiplier. Everyone starts at ×1. Every right answer adds 1. A wrong answer does not lower it. No cap.

Attack. After each question, pick a target on the leaderboard. If you answer the next question right, your target loses seconds left × 3 bricks. Your streak multiplier does not count on attacks. Your target stays until you change it. No target? A right answer saves the attack, and saved attacks all hit once you pick someone. Gang up on whoever is close to winning! Every hit lands in full. (Optional: set a per-question loss limit for castle walls.)

Final Round. Word problems: base bricks are doubled.

Win. The first castle to reach 20,000 bricks wins. If nobody gets there, the biggest castle wins.

Runs itself. Every screen has a timer and moves on by itself. Use Pause (top right) to stop and talk.

Tip: test with one Chromebook before class. Open the student link, join, and make sure the name appears here.
